# Currency Tools

Sift includes several market views backed by poe.ninja data. They are meant for quick economic context, not as a guarantee that a trade route is executable.

## Currency Trends

The **Currency Trends** page shows historical movement for currency items.

Use it to:

- Compare current chaos/exalt values.
- Inspect 7-day rate of change.
- Filter to stronger movement signals.
- Scan sparklines before buying crafting materials.

## Currency Pulse

The **Pulse** page is aimed at league-start and fast-moving economy reads. It groups currencies into practical pressure categories such as anchors, crafting pressure, quality, sockets, and corruption.

Each row shows current value, recent movement, 7-day movement, a signal label, and a sparkline. Use it to spot where demand is building before you commit to a craft or bulk purchase.

## Currency Arbitrage

The **Arbitrage** page scans exchange routes and recipe-style opportunities.

Controls include:

- **Have / Want** currency filters.
- Minimum volume per hour.
- Maximum gold cost.
- Maximum recent movement.
- Refresh to reload current data.

Results show estimated edge, profit, divine-per-gold, volume, recent movement, and a short playbook.

::: warning
Always verify liquidity, seller availability, market movement, and gold cost in-game before acting. Arbitrage output is a market signal, not an execution guarantee.
:::
